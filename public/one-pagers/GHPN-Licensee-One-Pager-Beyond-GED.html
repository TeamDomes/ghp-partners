Beyond GED · Hudson, NY

The program: Beyond GED

For Opportunity Youth, ages 16–24, out of school and out of work. Most left school to support their families, so the program pays them to learn: a tutoring stipend, a hot meal on study days, job placement, and a staff member who clears what stands in the way, from IDs to court dates.

GHPN has served Hudson since 2011 and is an independent 501(c)(3).
